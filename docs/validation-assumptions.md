# Validation assumptions

These are safe MVP defaults or unresolved operational facts, not permanent fleet policy and not legal advice. Change configurable values only with named operational ownership and test evidence.

| Decision area | Current safe position | Evidence needed before production policy is frozen |
|---|---|---|
| Fleet shape | Optimize role workflows for about 25 commercial trucks while keeping asset types extensible | Current vehicles, trailers, service vehicles, equipment, locations, and growth plan |
| GatorHub roster | Treat the checked-in 27-row `fleet.csv` as the import allowlist; preserve truck numbers as strings and exclude local scenario rows `107`, `112`, `115`, and `120` until adjudicated | Owner-approved comparison with the production GatorHub database, including every addition, rename, merge, and retirement |
| PM starting state | Never infer prior service from a current odometer or plan-creation date; a missing last-service date/value makes the initial service visibly due | Approved package tasks, interval/grace/reset rules, current trusted meters, and last-service evidence for every truck/package |
| PM dispatch effect | Due-soon/due/overdue is informational and does not itself place a truck out of service | Written fleet policy identifying any PM conditions that restrict dispatch and the authorized override path |
| Inspection obligations | Templates are versioned and configurable; no template is labeled universally FMCSA-required | Vehicle classifications, interstate/intrastate operation, current forms, and qualified compliance review |
| Record retention | Preserve operational history; do not treat an application default as a legal retention schedule | Approved schedule covering maintenance, defect DVIR, annual inspection, employment, privacy, litigation hold, and disposal |
| Out-of-service authority | Company OOS and government-declared OOS are distinct; RTS needs an authorized verifier and reasoned exception path | Written authority matrix and safety-blocker policy |
| Meter authority | Manual entry remains available; telematics becomes current only after identity, unit, time, monotonicity, and plausibility checks | Representative vehicle readings compared with dashboard/shop records over time |
| Meter plausibility | `METER_MAX_MILES_PER_HOUR=100.0`, `METER_MAX_ENGINE_HOURS_PER_HOUR=1.25`, and `METER_FUTURE_TOLERANCE_SECONDS=300` are configurable quarantine thresholds, not facts about every asset | Per-asset class operating envelope, device clock behavior, rollover/replacement procedures, and actual outliers |
| Retirement evidence | Retirement requires a separate free-text disposition and snapshots the current accepted reading for every active odometer/engine-hour meter; assets without an active cumulative meter can retire without meter evidence | Approved disposition vocabulary, authoritative retirement meters by asset class, and policy for failed/unreadable meters |
| AutoPi | Optional adapter only; exact device model, firmware, signals, cadence, buffering, and own-server path remain unknown | Registered hardware inventory and recorded payloads from representative trucks |
| DTC alert rules | A provisional built-in list of nine J1939 SPN/FMI codes (`backend/integrations/diagnostics.py`) raises human-reviewed alerts at `critical`/`warning`; unlisted codes are recorded as evidence without alerting; `DTC_ALERT_COOLDOWN_HOURS=24` silences the same code on the same asset after an alert is closed. No alert ever creates a work order | Fault-code history from deployed AutoPi hardware, maintenance-manager review of which codes warrant attention and at what urgency, observed alert-to-action conversion, and cooldown tuning; an editable rule table only once that evidence exists. Licensing researched 2026-09-05: no permissively-licensed SPN dictionary of useful size exists — `pretty_j1939` is Apache-2.0 but its bundled `J1939db.json` carries only ~11 SPNs, covering rules 100/110/168/175/190 and not 111/94/1761; isobus.net is ISO copyright and not redistributable; the only legal bulk paths are the SAE J1939DA or a commercial DBC, and neither should be bought yet. If the deployed box forwards DM1 lamp status, request it at the frame level (one lamp state per message, not per code) as the escalation path for unlisted codes |
| Component identity | Kinds are engine, transmission, reefer unit, APU, axle, aftertreatment, other; a serial is unique per `(organization, kind)`. Meter evidence at install/remove is best-effort — a meter with no accepted reading is omitted rather than blocking the technician — while retirement stays strict. Legacy `specs.equipment.*.serial_number` values are backfilled as best-available identity, not proof, and placeholders (`N/A`, `?`, anything under four characters) are skipped and printed for hand entry | Roster component serials and manufacturers, observed cross-kind serial collisions, whether mis-keyed kinds actually occur (narrowing the key later needs a row merge), and which kinds the fleet genuinely tracks |
| Offline cache | `OFFLINE_CACHE_HOURS=24`; foreground reconnect is correctness-critical and background sync is only an optimization | Disconnected-shift tests on managed target phones/tablets and device-loss risk review |
| Offline inventory | Central-stock changes require server confirmation; constrained offline issue/return must not claim authoritative availability | Warehouse/service-truck custody, connectivity observations, and approved negative-stock policy |
| Inventory topology | Schema supports warehouses and bins without assuming the number of stockrooms or service trucks | Physical location/bin map and ownership/custody rules |
| Inventory costing | Operational unit cost supports work-order decisions; it is not accounting-grade valuation | Accounting platform, valuation policy, reconciliation owner, and export/import contract |
| Count-variance approval | `INVENTORY_COUNT_VARIANCE_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD=0.00` requires independent `inventory.adjust` approval for every nonzero gross variance value submitted by a counter; value is the sum of absolute quantity variance times each part's default unit cost captured with the count | Written delegation, operating currency, valuation basis, tolerances by part/location, and whether quantity-only thresholds are also required |
| PO approval | `PO_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD=1000.00` is only a configurable working default | Written delegation, emergency purchase rules, currency, tax/freight treatment, and separation-of-duties review |
| Parts identity | Internal number plus alternate/manufacturer/vendor references; duplicate cleanup remains data-dependent | Parts export, label/barcode samples, vendor catalogs, and duplicate resolution rules |
| Technical manuals and OCR | Technical documents are versioned immutable PDFs with manager-recorded security-review references. Embedded text is indexed first; the supported worker uses PDFium plus English Tesseract for scans. Search excerpts remain citations, not maintenance instructions or automatic repairs. | Actual owner/service/parts/torque/wiring manuals, documented ownership/license, source-asset and engine applicability, malware-scan procedure, representative faded/table/diagram scans, measured OCR accuracy for torque values and part numbers, and technician review of page citations |
| CSV migration | MVP CSV import is limited to asset master rows with independent accepted/rejected results; parts, vendors, opening stock, and historical transactions are not bulk-loaded without an approved source contract | Actual source exports, field mapping, duplicate policy, opening-balance cutoff, rejected-row ownership, and reconciliation totals/sample histories |
| Barcode capture | MVP accepts keyboard-wedge scanner input or manual text containing a supported part, manufacturer, alternate, vendor, or barcode identifier; it does not require a camera-scanning library | Target scanner/camera models, dirty/damaged label samples, encoded identifier policy, lighting/glove trials, and observed manual-fallback success |
| Attachments | 10 MiB file limit and an allowlist of JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF, text, and CSV; recent unlinked offline staging is capped at 25 files/50 MiB per user and purged after the 24-hour default TTL. Commercial legacy attachments start financial/fail-closed until a reviewer creates an operational copy where justified. | Real inspection/repair files, disconnected-shift size/counts, retention, privacy, malware-scanning requirement, cleanup cadence, storage growth, and a reviewed legacy commercial-document classification inventory |
| Identity | Local authentication is the MVP authority; privileged system/integration administrators require MFA | Identity provider, joiner/mover/leaver workflow, device policy, and SSO/OIDC decision |
| Login throttling | Defaults count five failed password/TOTP attempts in 300 seconds per account and client, then impose a 900-second temporary lockout; identifiers are stored only as keyed hashes | Shared-client/NAT behavior, observed false lockouts and attack volume, support escalation, monitoring/retention, and approved threshold owner |
| Organizations | All operational data is organization-scoped; multi-organization administration is not part of the starting workflow | Commercialization/affiliate boundaries and formal tenant threat review |
| Webhooks | HTTPS and public destinations only by default; HTTP/private networks remain disabled | Approved receivers, egress policy, DNS/rebinding controls, signing-secret rotation, and retry ownership |
| Time | `America/Chicago` is the working display timezone; stored event time remains timezone-aware | Actual organization/location timezones and daylight-saving reporting rules |
| Backups | GPG AES-256 encrypts the checksummed database, attachment, and configuration unit; restore requires an owner-only passphrase file, rejects unsafe/bounded archives, and targets a clean database/media store; provisional target is 24-hour RPO and same-business-day restore | Leadership-approved RPO/RTO, passphrase custody/rotation and escrow, retention/offsite provider, measured archive/expanded-media limits, target capacity, and recorded drill cadence |
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA is the target; automated checks supplement rather than replace manual review | Keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, glove/touch, and field-device sessions with representative users |
| Integrations outside maintenance | Accounting, payroll, ELD/HOS, dispatch, fuel cards, CRM, and route planning remain external | Only a documented interface requirement can add an adapter; these do not become native MVP modules |

## Pilot evidence required

Before broad production rollout, collect three months of maintenance and parts transactions, current PM sheets and inspection forms, representative purchase orders/partial receipts, sample AutoPi messages, a physical inventory/location map, target-device connectivity observations, and interviews or observed work with every role.

The release validation record should name who approved inspection policy, OOS/RTS authority, meter precedence, purchasing thresholds, offline inventory behavior, retention, backup objectives, and external-system boundaries. Until then, the defaults above remain reversible safeguards rather than claims about the fleet.
